# Co

## Organization — *personal · professional · collaborative*

**Co organizes what you know and what you do — and it is yours.** Your notes become a connected graph, your projects become a board, your community gains a shared space — and a local AI assistant helps with all of it, without ever taking it away from you.

> **Start now, free** — create right here on the board, no login (up to 100 entries). When you want to **save, grow and invite people**, tap **Sign In** at the top.

## What you organize

- **Personal** — your notes, ideas, your life: a *second brain* where everything connects and the AI helps you think.
- **Professional** — your projects and your work: a *board* (kanban), a *garden* (wiki), tasks linked to knowledge, full history.
- **Collaborative** — your team, your community: *shared universes* — invitations, comments and real time.

…in the end it is all **Organization** — at any scale, and **always yours**.

## How it works

- **Write** in Markdown or **talk** with the assistant — Co connects your ideas and shows them as a board, a garden, a timeline and more.
- The **AI is local and private** by default; it only uses the cloud if *you* choose to.
- It runs **on your computer** or on our infra — **your data is always yours**, and you can take it with you at any time.
