## CO-540 — stale-if-error on public reads (self-host availability backstop)

Public-static reads now carry `stale-if-error=604800` (in addition to the existing
`s-maxage=60, stale-while-revalidate=86400`), so a CDN edge keeps the public site up
by serving the last-good cached version for up to 7 days when the self-host origin is
unreachable (asleep / power-out / offline) — "return latest if unavailable".

### Why
The M4 becomes sole prod with the UPS deferred; the edge cache is the availability
backstop. Pairs with Cloudflare "Always Online". The dynamic/auth/API surface stays
uncacheable and is unavailable while the origin is down (accepted tradeoff; full
failover is the deferred CO-539).
