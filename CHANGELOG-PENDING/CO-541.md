## CO-541 — changelog page can't scroll

Fixed the standalone changelog viewer (`/changelog`) being locked to the
viewport. Its inline `body{}` rule omitted overflow handling and so inherited
the global `style.css` `html,body{height:100%}` + `body{overflow:hidden}`,
making any content below the fold unreachable.

### Why
The changelog viewer loads `/style.css` (the app shell stylesheet) whose
`overflow:hidden` is intentional for the SPA but wrong for this long,
scrollable document page. Added `height:auto; overflow-y:auto;` (keeping
`min-height:100vh`) to the page's own `body{}` so it grows and scrolls.
