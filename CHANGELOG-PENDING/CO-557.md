## CO-557 — Routing: /<slug> ↔ /en/<slug>, anon default pt + Accept-Language

Both `/<slug>` (pt) and `/en/<slug>` (en) now resolve, mapping cleanly to the
slug-parallel content twins.

- **`/en/<slug>`** (and bare `/en`) → 307 to `/template/en/<slug>` when the en
  mirror entry exists, persisting `co_lang=en`. A slug with **no en mirror falls
  back to the pt page** (`/template/<slug>`) rather than 404 — slugs always
  resolve, language degrades gracefully. The static `/en/` prefix wins over the
  `/{slug}/{*subpath}` SPA wildcard.
- **`/<slug>`** is unchanged (`/sobre` → `/template/sobre`).
- **Anonymous default = pt**: with no `co_lang` cookie the server resolves from
  `Accept-Language` (`en*` → en, else pt) and persists it (pre-existing
  `detect_lang_from_accept`/`co_lang` path, now the en routes feed into it).
- Integration tests (real-binary `TestServer`): `/en/sobre` → `/template/en/sobre`
  + `co_lang=en`, `/en/termos` → pt fallback, `/sobre` unchanged, and the en
  mirror entry is present with `language: en`.
- New page routes catalogued in `docs/architecture/api-catalog.md` (under the
  backtick-free "SPA + page routes" heading) + `npm run openapi:gen` clean.

### Why
The navigation/negotiation glue over CO-555 (content mirror) and CO-556 (UI
strings): a visitor toggling or arriving with `Accept-Language: en` lands on the
English twin, and every page links to its exact counterpart.
