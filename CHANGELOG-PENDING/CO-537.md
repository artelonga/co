## CO-537 — Local disk pre-flight for self-host (disk-gate-selfhost.sh)

`scripts/disk-gate-selfhost.sh` is the self-host analogue of the CO-446 Fly disk
gate, but pure local `df` — no flyctl, no SSH. It guards against the SQLITE_FULL
crash-loop where a boot-time migration can't write `schema_version` on a near-full
volume.

- `disk-gate-selfhost.sh [DATA_DIR]` (default `$CO_WEB_DATA`, else `~/.co/data`).
- **Blocks (exit 1) above 85% full** with an actionable message (prune/relocate/
  extend + the Disk-full recovery runbook), **warns above 75%**, otherwise passes.
  Thresholds overridable via `BLOCK_PCT`/`WARN_PCT`.
- Resolves the nearest existing parent when the data dir doesn't exist yet (first
  boot), and reports human-readable free space.
