## CO-98 — Universe children: list helper + "Explorar" home panel

Builds on the existing `parent_key` column (already migrated) to surface a
universe's direct children for discovery.

### What changed

- **Storage helper**: `list_children_of_universe(parent)` returns the direct
  children of a universe (rows whose `parent_key` matches), excluding
  soft-deleted/archived rows. Panic-free — logs and returns an empty `Vec` on
  any prepare/query error.
- **Template home "Explorar" panel**: when `renderUniverseHome` finds no
  `index.md` but the universe has children (filtered from `state.userUniverses`
  by `parent_key`), it renders an "Explorar este universo" card list instead of
  the generic empty-state hint. Anonymous visitors to a parent universe now see
  its children listed.
- **Styles**: `.universe-home-explorar*` rules for the panel.
- **Storage tests**: `test_parent_key_round_trip` (set/read parent_key) and
  `test_list_children_of_universe` (tree-list excludes deleted children).

### Why

A parent universe with no `index.md` previously showed only a generic empty
state, hiding its sub-universes. Listing children turns the parent's home into a
discovery surface without introducing a separate "category" abstraction.
