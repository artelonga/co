## CO-520 — Contract-test tier (the lib-vs-integration safety net)

The missing test layer between unit-tests-with-fakes and the browser e2e: a tier that
pins every **bot ↔ co-web** cross-process seam against the actual co-web source, so a
drift on either side fails CI instead of breaking in prod (the gap the wave review
exposed — LGPD erase/forget, the digest param, the dead window fallback).

- `tools/whatsapp-bot/tests/test_co_contract.py` — generalized into **21 contract
  tests** pinning the bot's CoTools request/response shapes against the real Rust:
  me/export · me/erase + me/forget (the exact `confirm` phrases vs `whatsapp_me.rs`
  consts) · telemetry/digest (`days` param, not `window`) · consent (`operator`) ·
  onboarding + link/verify bodies · the digest response fields. Literals are hardcoded
  in the test (not imported from the bot) and cross-checked against the co-web source on
  disk — proven to FAIL on 9 deliberate drifts (field renames, method demount, phrase
  change, the CO-518 `days`/`window` bug shape), in both directions.
- `docs/CONTRACT-TESTS.md` — documents the tier: what it is, why (the lib-vs-integration
  gap), the rule (every seam pinned against source), and how to add a contract test.

### Why
CI-green ≠ correct: both sides were unit-tested with fakes, so cross-process mismatches
passed CI and broke in production. This tier makes that class of bug fail loudly.
(The contract suite lives in the bot — non-git — and the tier doc in co; folds into the
agent runtime when the bot becomes a thin client, CO-507.)
