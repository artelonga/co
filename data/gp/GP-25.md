THEN:
- [ ] `POST /api/v1/universes` creates universe with: name, description, visibility, map_data, theme, rules

- [ ] `GET /api/v1/universes` lists universes (filtered by visibility: public + own private)

- [ ] Owner auto-set from JWT user_id on create
- [ ] Visibility enum: `individual` (only owner), `private` (invited), `public` (everyone)
