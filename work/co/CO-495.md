GIVEN the front door is for someone whose comfort zone is WhatsApp but who is eager
to understand the technology underneath — and deserves dignity, not a funnel,
WHEN the user interacts and (optionally) asks what's behind the surface,
THEN the bot is warm, plain, jargon-free, repair-friendly, and offers **one
look-behind door at a time**, each teaching a real concept (local AI, data you can
hold, open source, no lock-in) — never required, paced to curiosity.

## Acceptance

- [ ] A general, warm, jargon-free `bot/profile` persona (no named person) — see
  `docs/whatsapp-bot-profile.example.json`. Never names "universe/API/token";
  uses "seu cantinho / seu jardim"; never phrases legal text (defers to CO-491).
- [ ] Progressive-disclosure intents: `como funciona` (local AI), `mostra minhas
  coisas` (data you can hold → CO-493), `isso é seguro` / open-source (`a receita é
  aberta`), `e se eu quiser sair` (portability). Each a self-contained rung.
- [ ] The **honest boundary** line (WhatsApp = Meta; content transits them, saved
  data doesn't) surfaced at onboarding — verbatim from CO-491 `boundary`.
- [ ] Repair on confusion (no blame); remembers context (no re-explaining).
- [ ] No dark patterns anywhere; the exit is always frictionless (CO-493).
- [ ] First proactive touch is warmth (the birthday greeting, CO-487), not an ask.

## Notes
- North star ≠ engagement. Success = ownership felt, a little of the magic
  understood, trust by verification, could-leave-but-stays, brought someone in.
  (`ñandé`, not `oré`.) See `docs/whatsapp-launch.md` → UX north star.
