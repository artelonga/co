GIVEN output B of the synthesis loop is a **lead-temperature digest** built from
CO's own `telemetry_events`/analytics — joined to the per-user identity from
CO-490 — that classifies each lead as cold/warm/hot with a trend and a *why*
(which pages, whether they returned, which language), and LGPD requires that an
anonymous visitor never becomes a nominal lead,
WHEN telemetry exists for identified/consented people it is reported **named**;
when telemetry is anonymous it is reported only in **aggregate**,
THEN the bot can answer "who's warming up?" with consented, attributable insight
for known leads and non-identifying totals for everyone else — never manufacturing
a person from anonymous traffic.

## Acceptance

- [ ] co-web **digest query** that reads `telemetry_events`/analytics and emits, per
  identified lead: temperature (**cold/warm/hot**), **trend** (rising/flat/cooling),
  and **why** (top pages, return-visit signal, language) over a window.
- [ ] **Identity join (CO-490):** named rows ONLY for users whose identity is known
  and consented; events with no consented identity are folded into **aggregate**
  counts. A nominal lead is **never** synthesized from anonymous telemetry (LGPD).
- [ ] Bot **telemetry-digest capability** (routed to by CO-498) that renders the
  digest as a WhatsApp message: named leads first (temp + trend + why), then an
  anonymous-aggregate footer.
- [ ] Respects Do-Not-Track / `dados-rastreados.md` posture — opted-out users
  contribute nothing, not even to aggregate where the policy forbids it.
- [ ] Tests: cold/warm/hot thresholds, trend computation, named-vs-aggregate split,
  and the "anonymous never named" invariant. `CHANGELOG-PENDING/CO-499.md`.

## Notes
- B is the read-only output of the loop — it *reports* on the substrate, it doesn't
  mutate it (contrast A/Jardim and C/Tarefas which write).
- Temperature thresholds and the trend window are config, not hard-coded — they
  vary per universe/missão.
