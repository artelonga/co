GIVEN anything the assistant writes or organizes **for** a user is **that user's
data** under LGPD — the model is a lens over the user's substrate, never the owner
of what it produces for them,
WHEN the bot curates content (notes, summaries, links it creates) into a user's
space,
THEN that content is persisted in the user's universe, included verbatim in their
export and erasure, and removed when they revoke — no LLM-generated artifact about
a user lives outside the user's control.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Bot-curated content is written to the user's own universe (not a side store),
  tagged as assistant-curated so provenance is clear (CO-74 `source` relation).
- [ ] `mostra minhas coisas` → a complete export of the user's space (their entries
  **and** assistant-curated content) delivered as a file in WhatsApp (over the
  existing Vault/static export). See CO-493.
- [ ] `apaga tudo` and `esquece de mim` remove curated content too (erasure is
  total; revoke drops the bot's per-user token).
- [ ] Optionally retain the consent + interaction provenance the user can inspect
  ("o que você sabe sobre mim?") — their data, their access (LGPD Art. 18 I/II).
- [ ] Export/erasure are deterministic and complete (test: curated entry appears in
  export and is gone after erasure).

## Notes
- Pairs with CO-491 (consent record is also the user's data) and CO-493 (the
  commands). The principle: the user can *see, take, and leave with* everything the
  system holds about them, including model output.
