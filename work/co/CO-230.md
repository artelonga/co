## Goal

Unify the three current auth-gating styles (middleware layer, middleware+token, in-handler `resolve_role()`) behind a single typed extractor hierarchy so that auth requirements are expressed in handler signatures and missing gates become compile errors.

## Children

- CO-222 — Unify auth gating into a single typed extractor

## Acceptance

Every protected handler uses `AuthenticatedUser` or `OwnerOnly` extractor; no in-handler `resolve_role()` calls remain.
