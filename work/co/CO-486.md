GIVEN local `meta.db` grew to **156 MB** while per-universe content is only
**23.5 MB total** — with just 29 `telemetry_events` rows and **0 freelist pages**
(so `VACUUM` alone won't shrink it; the bloat is derived/append-only data, not
free space) — and `meta.db` shares the **1 GB prod `co_data` volume**, this is the
next CO-446 disk-fill incident waiting to happen,
WHEN we identify the growth table(s) and apply bounded retention + periodic
compaction,
THEN `meta.db` stays small and predictable, the prod volume can't fill, and the
CO-484 bundle exports from a clean store.

## Acceptance

- [ ] **Diagnose:** `dbstat` breakdown of `meta.db` by table (prime suspects:
  `entry_versions` history, `entry_kb_*` warehouse, `entries_fts`). Record the
  actual culprit(s).
- [ ] **Retention:** cap the offending append-only data — e.g. keep last N
  `entry_versions` per entry and prune older; rotate/cap the KB warehouse;
  rebuild/optimize FTS. Plus a scheduled `VACUUM` (incremental where possible).
- [ ] A startup + periodic job enforces it; current `meta.db` size exported to
  `/metrics`.
- [ ] **Invariant:** add a `meta.db < threshold` check to `scripts/smoke-prod.sh`
  and the pre-deploy gate (alongside the `df -P /data > 85%` block).
- [ ] Migration is self-contained (no assuming earlier-version schema — the
  guarded-migration lesson); safe on a near-full disk (CO-446 pre-flight).

## Out of scope / follow-ups

- Moving telemetry/analytics to a separate store (the CO-123 ClickHouse plan).
- Per-universe `data.db` retention (these are small today; revisit if they grow).
