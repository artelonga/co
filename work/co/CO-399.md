## As

a brain owner working across several universes at once, I need ONE sala whose
scope is all my universes — or any subset I pick — so the canvas matches how I
actually think: across containers, not inside one.

## I Need

`/sala` (scope = every universe visible to the caller) and `/sala?u=a,b,…`
(explicit subset), rendered by the SAME `shared/sala.html` surface per
`docs/architecture/sala-surface.md`. Nodes carry their universe key; state
persists per (scope-hash, user).

## Context

> **Constraint:** ONE surface — extend `shared/sala.html` scope parsing
> (it already branches on `/u/{key}/sala` vs `/sala/{token}`); do NOT fork
> the page or grow a canvas anywhere else. `workspace_states` extends its
> key (scope column), not a new table. Meta-DB next free migration: check
> `work/co/CLAUDE.md` claim protocol (v74+ at time of writing).

## Acceptance

- [ ] `GET /sala` serves the sala surface with scope = caller-visible universes
- [ ] `GET /sala?u=a,b` limits scope to the listed keys (visibility-gated per key)
- [ ] Entry picker offers entries from every universe in scope, labeled by universe
- [ ] Cross-universe edges persist with `key::path` notation (canonical link syntax)
- [ ] `workspace_states` rows key on scope (single-universe rows unchanged — additive migration)
- [ ] Share tokens work for multi-universe scopes (read-only, visibility re-checked at read)
- [ ] E2E: two-universe subset sala renders nodes from both; anon sees only public scope

- Yggdrasil renderer of multi-scope (follows the engine, later)
