## As

a CO user trusting the platform with private content

## I Need

per-universe AEAD encryption of content at rest + in transit, **and** a hardened privileged compute zone that can decrypt narrowly to produce aggregations (never raw rows), with audit logs and k-anonymity-enforced egress

## To

make the privacy claim in `data/universes/template/content/privacidade.md` **literally true** — operators cannot read individual user content, even with full server access — without sacrificing the analytics surface entirely

## Stories

- CO-130 — Privileged compute zone (separate Fly app/region, locked-down ingress)
- CO-131 — Aggregation job allow-list + k-anonymity DLP at egress
- CO-132 — Key-access audit log (write-only from zone, read-only from `co-web`)

## Acceptance gates

- [ ] `flyctl ssh console -a co-artelonga` → reading `/data/universes/<u>/content/*.co` shows ciphertext, never plaintext
- [ ] Privileged zone outbound: only destinations are `events.aggregated.*` topics; no other egress paths
- [ ] An aggregation job that would emit a row with `count < 5` is rejected at the egress filter, with audit trail
- [ ] Every `K_u` unwrap creates an audit row; tampering with the audit log is detected
- [ ] Recovery flow (CO-106) lets a user with lost password regain access via escrow

## Honest tradeoff (load-bearing)

This **is not zero-trust analytics**. Inside the privileged zone, Flink decrypts user events to compute aggregations. Raw decrypted data never leaves the zone, only k-anonymous aggregations do. The five hardening controls (network isolation, audit log, DLP at egress, job allow-list, time-bounded keys) are what makes "operator cannot read" defensible. True zero-trust analytics requires either confidential compute (Nitro Enclaves) or homomorphic encryption — see CO-research-HE-FP for the FP/HE review and the partial-HE plan that may complement this in later phases.

## Out of scope

- Full FHE-based queryable encrypted analytics — research-grade, see HE+FP review
- Confidential compute (Nitro Enclaves) on Fargate — Phase 5+ revisit
- Homomorphic encryption for arbitrary metrics — partial-HE for narrow counters is in scope; full-HE is not

## Why now

Tier 4 of the roadmap. Encryption at rest is the headline privacy claim; without the privileged zone hardening it's unsubstantiated. Ship together or not at all.
