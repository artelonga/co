## 2) The two model heads — what they do today

Both live in `src/strategies/`. The `RFQ_SELIC_OPERATOR_ENABLED=true` boot gate is required for *either* SELIC head (it's the wiring switch in `main.rs:393`); the actual head is selected by `RFQ_SELIC_STRATEGY`.

### `OperatorSelicStrategy` (`operator_selic.rs`) — pre-event regime
- Pure gaussian over the 25 bp grid: `P(strike) = N(strike; center, σ)` discretised.
- One markup applied identically YES + NO. No asymmetry. No caps.
- Strikes with `P < strike_min_p` reject `LowConfidence`.
- **Best for**: pre-announcement (operator has wide belief, σ ≈ 50–60 bp).

### `SelicConvictionStrategy` (`selic_conviction.rs`) — post-event regime, **active in current Fly secrets**
- Same gaussian for *favorable*-side pricing only (modal NO + off-modal YES).
- Hard structure — *favorable* side capped per-distance: `[None, 100, 10, 1]` = modal uncapped, ±25 bp 100, ±50 bp 10, ±75+ 1.
- *Loss* side (modal YES + off-modal NO) is rejected unless `RFQ_HEDIX_INCENTIVE_ENABLED=true` and the budget has room. Loss-side fills price at a flat floor (`incentive_quote_cents`, default 25¢, with R$1 fill-floor adjustment by qty).
- **Self-organizing balance**: `RFQ_TOTAL_CASH_CENTS` × `RFQ_HEDIX_INCENTIVE_RATIO` derives the incentive budget; remainder is "mm-performance" (informational).
- Loss-side spend tracking is in-memory `RwLock<i64>` per process — **the durable record is the `hedix_incentive-YYYY-MM-DD.jsonl` ledger** (`selic_conviction.rs:185`). Restarts and the second Fly machine each reset the counter to 0; under heavy incentive flow the budget can be exceeded by up to N× (N = active machines + restarts). For the launch volume this is acceptable; flagging it for prod risk review.

### What changes for June
- **Modal will likely shift** — depends on COPOM 279 announcement (or your forecast if pre-announcement). Update `RFQ_SELIC_OPERATOR_CENTER_BPS` and `RFQ_SELIC_CONVICTION_MODAL_BPS` together.
- **Pre-event vs post-event regime**: if the gateway will quote in the trading window *before* the announcement (Hedix opens markets at `start_date`, which is post-event — the canonical pattern from APR27 was 30h gap event→start), `conviction` with current `dispersion_bps=25` is fine. If the operator wants to quote the runup to the announcement, switch to `operator` with σ≈50 bp.
- **Catalyst kill** will arm automatically once `event_at` is in the YAML — RFQs within ±1h reject `MARKET_CLOSED`.
