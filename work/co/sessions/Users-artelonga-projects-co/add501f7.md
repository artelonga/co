- **DB v7 migration** (`universe_pool.rs`): Creates `references_meta` (structured shadow table with medium/seed_status/blob_sha256 columns and indexes) + `references_fts` (FTS5 over title/body/transcription).

- **REST API** (`reference_routes.rs`): Full CRUD + `GET /references?medium=pdf` with FTS, `GET /references/orphan-blobs`, `GET /references/broken-cards`.
