## As

A visitor (logged or anonymous) landing on the template universe (`/` or `/template`)

## I Need

The template's `index.md` to read like a wiki article — short prose, organic hyperlinks to every other entry in the template universe, plus a "Ver mais" dynamic section listing the most-accessed (or most-popular) titles

## So That

New users immediately understand what CO is, what they can explore, AND have a single discoverable landing that links to all the seeded content (security docs, infra, sobre, termos, conta-e-mensagens, etc.) without having to dig through a sidebar

## Context

- **Why**: today template's home is a Kanban board with 9 tutorial tasks. The "Conteúdo" view is the closest thing to a wiki but isn't the default. Visitors don't see the *body of knowledge* CO ships with.
- **Scope**:
  - Rewrite `co-web/seed/template/index.md` — make it the wiki article (the same content already exists scattered: `sobre.md`, `co-plataforma.md`, `guia.md`, `dados-rastreados.md`, `linhas-do-tempo.md`).
  - Add organic `[[wikilinks]]` or `[markdown links](/...)` to: sobre, termos, privacidade, dados-rastreados, linhas-do-tempo, co-plataforma, guia, seguranca, licensa, infra, infra-co, renderers, transaction-log.
  - **"Ver mais" section** — new dynamic SSR'd or client-fetched listing of the top-N entries by access count. Backend exposes a per-universe "popularity" view (CO-241's `body_chars` + entry_events frequency, or just `updated_at DESC` for v1).
  - Mention the **`co` development sub-universe** (a sibling universe with all the CO-N tasks) — link to it as "Veja o código por trás disso →".

## Acceptance

- Anonymous visitor at `/` sees the new wiki landing rendered by the `renderUniverseHome()` code path (i.e. served because there's no project selected, OR because we change the default home to render the wiki for template).
- Every link in `index.md` resolves to a 200 entry on the template universe.
- "Ver mais" section displays at least 5 entries with their titles, ordered by popularity heuristic.
- A link to `/co` (the CO dev universe) is present and labeled clearly.
- `co-web/e2e/seed-links.spec.ts` extended to verify links resolve.

## Blast radius

- ~150 LOC: seed/template/index.md rewrite + 1 new API endpoint `GET /api/v1/universes/<slug>/entries/popular` + ~30 LOC SPA fetch in `boot.js renderUniverseHome()`.
- Backward-compatible — no schema changes; popularity ranking uses existing `entry_events` table.
