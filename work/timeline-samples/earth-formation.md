# Formation of Earth

About **4.6 billion years ago**, dust and gas in the solar nebula coalesced into our planet through gravitational accretion. The early Earth was a hellscape:

- Surface temperature: well above 1000 °C
- Atmosphere: hydrogen + helium, soon lost to solar wind
- No liquid water, no continents, no biosphere
- Heavy bombardment from leftover planetesimals continued for ~600 My

A Mars-sized body called *Theia* collided with proto-Earth around 4.5 Gya, ejecting debris that coalesced into the Moon.

> Geological era: **Hadean** (4.6–4.0 Gya)
