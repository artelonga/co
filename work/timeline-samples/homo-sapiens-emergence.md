# Emergence of Homo sapiens

Roughly **300,000 years ago**, anatomically modern humans (*Homo sapiens*) emerge in Africa. Key fossil sites:

- **Jebel Irhoud, Morocco** (~315,000 BP) — earliest known modern human fossils
- **Omo Kibish, Ethiopia** (~195,000 BP) — long the standard "first H. sapiens" site
- **Florisbad, South Africa** (~260,000 BP)

This period overlaps with:
- The Middle Stone Age (lithic technology)
- Habitual control of fire
- Probable origins of complex symbolic thought
- Coexistence with Neanderthals, Denisovans, and possibly other archaic humans

> Approximate population: 10,000–100,000 individuals across Africa
