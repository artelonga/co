# 300,000 years from now (year 302,026 CE)

A symmetric counterpoint to the emergence of *Homo sapiens*. **300,000 years from now** is as far in the future as anatomically modern humans are in the past.

Possible considerations:

- Genetic drift + selection over 12,000 generations: substantial morphological divergence likely
- If technological civilization persists: data integrity over deep time becomes a structural concern
- If it doesn't: rebuilt civilizations may have no record of the 21st century at all
- Climate shifts (Milankovitch cycles): several glaciation/interglacial transitions
- The Sun is ~3% brighter than today; Earth slightly warmer baseline

> What records, languages, or content forms persist over deep time is an open question. Co's `.co` protobuf format (CO-86) targets durability; whether that matters at 300,000-year scale is an interesting design question.
